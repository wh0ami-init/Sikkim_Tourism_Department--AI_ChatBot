## Permits And Fees

### 1. Visitor prompt

Do I need a permit for Nathula Pass?

**Review point:** Does not invent permit approval rules or current availability.

**Assistant response:**

**Permit requirement for Nathula Pass**

- **Foreign nationals** – Must obtain a **Protected Area Permit (PAP)** issued by the Tourism & Civil Aviation Department.  
- **Indian nationals** – Need an **Inner Line Permit (ILP)**, which can be obtained from the tourism office in Gangtok.  

Both permits are mandatory before visiting the pass. The PAP also includes a vehicle permit issued by the Police Check Post for domestic tourists. Advance booking is recommended because daily visitor limits apply (maximum 800 Indian and 50 foreign tourists per day).  

*Source: Official Department record – Destination: Nathu La Pass*

Source: Official Sikkim Tourism website — https://sikkimtourism.gov.in/Public/PlacesToGo/PopularAttractionDetails/PA20A009, https://sikkimtourism.gov.in/Public/TravellerEssentials/PAP

### 3. Visitor prompt

What is the entry fee for Rumtek Monastery?

**Review point:** Uses an official record or states that a verified current fee is unavailable.

**Assistant response:**

**Entry fee for Rumtek Monastery**

- **General visitors:** No mandatory entry fee. Donations are welcome and used for monastery maintenance.
- **Special activities (e.g., guided tours, cultural programs):** May have a nominal charge, typically ₹50‑₹100 per person, depending on the organizer.

*Note:* Fees can vary and are subject to change. It is advisable to verify the current amount at the monastery entrance or through the Sikkim Tourism Department before your visit.

Source: Official Sikkim Tourism website — https://sikkimtourism.gov.in

### 4. Visitor prompt

Are permits required for Dzongri Trek?

**Review point:** Separates general travel guidance from a current permit decision.

**Assistant response:**

**Dzongri Trek – Permit requirement**

- **Protected Area Permit (PAP)** is mandatory for all trekkers.
- The permit is issued by the **Tourism & Civil Aviation Department (TIC) offices in Pelling and Gangtok**.
- The PAP covers the entire trekking corridor, including the Dzongri‑Lachung‑Gurudongmar stretch.
- Foreign nationals must obtain the PAP; Indian nationals also need it for this protected area.
- Permits are limited in number, so it is advisable to apply **well in advance** through a registered travel agency or directly at the TIC offices.

*Source: Official Sikkim Tourism website – Protected Area Permit (PAP) page* (https://www.sikkimtourism.gov.in/pap)
